# Co-Financing and Revenue Share Agreement

This Agreement is made on [__________] between **Lanternfield Pictures Ltd** ("Producer") and **Corvane Capital LLC** ("Investor") in respect of the feature film provisionally titled "The Salt Road" (the "Film").

## 1. Budget

1.1 The approved budget of the Film is USD 5,000,000 as itemised in Schedule 1 (the "Approved Budget").

1.2 Any cost of producing and delivering the Film above the Approved Budget is an "Overage".

## 2. Funding

2.1 The Producer shall borrow USD 3,000,000 from Harbourline Bank (the "Bank") under a loan agreement between the Producer and the Bank (the "Loan Agreement"), secured against distributor minimum guarantees. The interest rate, fees and repayment dates are set out in the Loan Agreement.

2.2 The Investor shall pay USD 1,500,000 (the "Investment") to the Producer in three instalments on the dates set out in Schedule 2.

2.3 The Producer shall fund the balance of the Approved Budget, being USD 500,000 (the "Producer Contribution"), and shall fund any Overage.

2.4 The Investor shall have no obligation to fund any amount beyond the Investment.

## 3. Production and Rights

3.1 The Producer shall have sole creative and business control of the Film. The Investor shall have no approval or consultation rights.

3.2 The Producer shall own the copyright in the Film and all rights in it throughout the world in perpetuity.

3.3 The Investor shall receive an "Executive Producer" credit in the end titles.

## 4. Sales and Collection

4.1 The Producer may appoint a sales agent. The sales agent's commission shall not exceed 15% of Gross Receipts and its recoupable expenses shall not exceed USD 150,000 in aggregate.

4.2 "Gross Receipts" means all sums actually received from the exploitation of the Film in all media throughout the world.

4.3 All Gross Receipts shall be paid into a collection account operated by an independent collection agent appointed by the Producer (the "Collection Account").

## 5. Application of Gross Receipts

5.1 The collection agent shall apply Gross Receipts in the Collection Account in the following order:

(a) first, to the collection agent, its fee of 1% of Gross Receipts;

(b) second, to the sales agent, its commission and recoupable expenses within the limits in Clause 4.1;

(c) third, to the Bank, all principal, interest and fees due under the Loan Agreement until repaid in full. If the Gross Receipts are insufficient to pay any amount when due under the Loan Agreement, the Producer shall pay the shortfall from its own funds;

(d) fourth, to the Investor, until it has received 120% of the Investment actually paid (the "Investor Recoupment");

(e) fifth, to the Producer, until it has received the Producer Contribution, any Overage it funded and any shortfall it paid under paragraph (c);

(f) sixth, to the director and principal cast, the deferred fees set out in Schedule 3, not exceeding USD 200,000 in aggregate (the "Deferments");

(g) thereafter, the balance ("Net Profits") shall be paid 50% to the Investor and 50% to the Producer.

5.2 Any profit participation granted by the Producer to any director, writer or performer shall be paid solely out of the Producer's share of Net Profits.

## 6. Statements

6.1 The collection agent shall send both parties a statement of Gross Receipts and their application quarterly for the first three years after the Film's first release and semi-annually thereafter.

6.2 The Investor may audit the Collection Account once in each calendar year at its own cost. If an audit reveals an underpayment to the Investor of more than 5%, the Producer shall bear the cost of that audit.

## 7. Investor Default

7.1 If the Investor fails to pay any instalment of the Investment within 10 business days after the Producer's written notice of non-payment, then:

(a) the Investor Recoupment shall be reduced to 100% of the Investment actually paid; and

(b) the Investor's share of Net Profits shall be reduced in the proportion that the Investment actually paid bears to USD 1,500,000.

7.2 The Producer may then seek replacement finance on any terms. Any replacement financier may be paid in priority to the Investor.

## 8. Term

This Agreement continues for as long as any Gross Receipts are received.
