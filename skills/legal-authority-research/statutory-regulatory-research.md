# Statutory And Regulatory Research

Statutory and regulatory research starts with official text and tracks whether the text has changed.

## Required Checks

- Identify the exact statute, code, regulation, rule, or guidance.
- Use official or authoritative text when available.
- Record effective date, version date, amendments, repeals, sunset dates, and pending changes when visible.
- Check retroactivity, transition provisions, grandfathering, effective-date gaps, and whether the document facts fall before or after the relevant legal change.
- Check whether cited cases relied on older text.
- Separate statutory text from agency guidance and commentary.
- Preserve preemption, scope, exemption, and applicability questions.

## Statute Or Regulation Entry

```yaml
source_id: S-010
title: official title
section: section, article, rule, or regulation
jurisdiction: issuing jurisdiction
source_type: statute | regulation | rule | guidance | commentary
version_date: effective or accessed version date
proposition_supported: exact proposition
pinpoint: section, subsection, paragraph, or quoted passage
change_status: current | amended | repealed | pending | unknown
temporal_scope: prospective | retroactive | transitional | unknown
limits: scope, exemptions, missing facts, or counsel questions
```

No statutory proposition is confirmed unless the relevant text, version, and applicability limits are recorded.
