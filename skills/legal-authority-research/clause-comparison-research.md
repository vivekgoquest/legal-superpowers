# Clause Comparison Research

Clause comparison separates legal effect from drafting practice.

## Rules

- Quote the user clause before comparing it.
- Identify the clause's operative legal propositions.
- Compare similar clauses only after source, context, and document type are identified.
- Label sample clauses, form documents, negotiation guides, and market surveys as market-practice evidence.
- market practice is not legal authority.
- Do not turn common drafting language into an enforceability conclusion.

## Clause Comparison Entry

```yaml
clause_issue: issue name
user_clause_text: quoted clause or source location
legal_propositions:
  - claim IDs
comparable_patterns:
  - source, context, and pattern
market_practice_findings:
  - drafting norm, not legal authority
legal_authority_findings:
  - claim IDs tied to primary or persuasive authority
business_implications:
  - negotiation or operational concern
counsel_questions:
  - open legal questions
```

Use clause comparisons to improve issue spotting and negotiation framing. Do not use them as final legal conclusions.
