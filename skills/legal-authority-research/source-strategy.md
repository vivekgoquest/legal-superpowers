# Source Strategy

Use public and local sources conservatively. Disclose source access and limits in every packet.

## United States

- CourtListener for case-law discovery, opinions, and citation lookup.
- Eyecite for citation extraction and normalization.
- Federal Register and eCFR for federal regulations.
- congress.gov for federal legislative materials.
- Official state legislative and regulatory portals for state-law issues.

Limits: CourtListener and citation lookup can help verify existence, but they do not prove current good law or proposition support.

## United Kingdom

- The National Archives case law service.
- legislation.gov.uk.
- BAILII for public case-law discovery when official-source coverage is unavailable or incomplete, with limits disclosed.

## European Union

- EUR-Lex.
- Curia / CJEU materials.
- European Commission guidance for materials tied to the researched issue.

## India

- Supreme Court and High Court official sites for primary case-law text when available.
- Official legislative and regulatory portals.
- Indian Kanoon for discovery only unless checked against authoritative text.

## Unknown Or Unsupported Jurisdiction

Use this exact blocker:

```text
No approved source strategy exists for this jurisdiction yet. I can prepare a research plan and issue map, but confirmed legal propositions require counsel or an approved legal database/source strategy.
```

## Required Disclosure

State:

- Source access used.
- Source access unavailable.
- Whether citations were mechanically verified.
- Whether currentness was citator-verified.
- Whether proposition support was checked against source text.
