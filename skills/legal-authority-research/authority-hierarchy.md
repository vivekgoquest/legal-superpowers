# Authority Hierarchy

Create an authority hierarchy map before relying on sources.

## Source Classes

- Binding primary authority.
- Persuasive primary authority.
- Statutes, codes, and enacted law.
- Regulations and administrative rules.
- Agency guidance, enforcement materials, and official commentary.
- Court decisions by court level.
- Secondary sources.
- Market practice and sample documents.
- Background material.

## Rules

- The hierarchy is jurisdiction-specific.
- Binding authority beats persuasive authority.
- Primary authority beats secondary commentary.
- Official text beats unofficial summaries.
- Market practice and sample documents may inform drafting norms, but they do not prove legal enforceability.
- Cross-jurisdiction authority is analogy unless the jurisdiction treats it as binding or incorporated.

## Required Output

For each source, record:

- Jurisdiction.
- Source class.
- Binding, persuasive, secondary, market-practice, or background status.
- Why the source can or cannot support the proposition.
