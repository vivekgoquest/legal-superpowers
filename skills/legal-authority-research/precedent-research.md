# Precedent Research

Precedent research tests whether case law supports, limits, or refutes a legal proposition in the target jurisdiction.

## Required Checks

- Identify the precise legal proposition.
- Search binding courts first, then persuasive courts.
- Record court level, date, procedural posture, and key facts.
- Record whether the case is published, unpublished or non-precedential, sealed, withdrawn, vacated, or citation-restricted.
- Check whether the case is still current, overruled, abrogated, superseded, distinguished, or criticized.
- Search contrary authority before synthesis.
- Preserve factual limits and analogy limits.

## Case-Law Entry

```yaml
source_id: S-001
citation: reported or neutral citation
court: court name
date: decision date
jurisdiction: target jurisdiction
authority_level: binding | persuasive | secondary | background
publication_status: published | unpublished | non-precedential | withdrawn | unknown
proposition_supported: exact proposition
pinpoint: paragraph, page, section, or quoted passage
currentness_status: verified | not-verified | overruled | superseded | unknown
contrary_authority: source IDs or not found with method disclosed
limits: factual, procedural, or jurisdictional limits
```

If currentness is not citator-verified, the packet must say counsel should verify treatment in a legal research platform before relying on the case.

An unpublished or non-precedential case cannot be treated as binding unless the jurisdiction's rules permit that treatment and the packet records the rule.
