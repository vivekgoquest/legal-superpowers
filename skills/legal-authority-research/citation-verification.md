# Citation Verification

Citation verification prevents hallucinated or misused authority.

Citation existence does not prove proposition support.

## Required Checks

- Extract citations from user text, sources, and draft output.
- Normalize citations when possible.
- Verify that the cited source exists.
- Open or inspect the source text before relying on it.
- Match the pinpoint text to the proposition.
- Record whether the source is primary authority, secondary authority, market material, or background.
- Record currentness, negative treatment, or unverified status.

## Failure States

- Fake citation: cannot support any proposition.
- Real citation, unopened source: cannot support any confirmed proposition.
- Real source, wrong jurisdiction: analogy only unless hierarchy says otherwise.
- Real source, wrong proposition: source may remain in notes but claim is unsupported.
- Real source, old law: currentness or amendment question goes to counsel.

## Required Disclosure

Every packet must say whether citations were mechanically verified and whether proposition support was manually checked against source text.
